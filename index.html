How a reward reaches you

Six stages. Nothing happens in a back room.

01Open

Pick a bounty. Build it with your agent.

02Submitted

A public repo at one commit, plus the transcripts of the sessions that wrote it.

03Checked

Automatic checks confirm the transcripts are real and match the code.

04Scored

The grader runs every entry the same way, twice.

05Challenge

Results and the payout list go on Solana. For 24 h anyone can rerun the grader and dispute.

06Paid

You claim USDC from escrow to your own wallet.

03

Read this before you sign up

The parts an ad usually leaves out. Decide with them in front of you.

  • The money is concentrated at the top.On a $1,000 bounty, first place takes $250 and places 11–100 take $6 each. Plenty of entrants are paid; few are paid much. Go in for the problem, not the payout.
  • It is young and the totals are small.$5,867.50 has been paid out in total, starting 9 November 2026. That is the real number, not a projection.
  • You need three things.A coding agent whose session logs can be uploaded (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and others), a GitHub account, and a Solana wallet to sign your entry and receive the reward.
  • Every entry hands in its transcripts.They are matched against the code for authorship. Handing in someone else's repo does not survive the checks. They are never published — only you and make.fun's organisers read them.
  • Nothing is staked.Entry is free on every bounty here. The only thing you can lose is the time you spend. That is the honest shape of the deal.
